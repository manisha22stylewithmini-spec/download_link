          Your data, your call

          Grab works without an account. Here's exactly what happens with your info.

          • Stays on this deviceYour download history, searches and settings live in this browser's storage. We don't keep a copy.
          • Sent to fetch videosLinks and search words you enter go to our server only to find the video or results. Our host (Vercel) may keep standard request logs for a short time.
          • Personalized picks (optional)If you allow it, Grab looks at your searches and downloads on this device to suggest topics in your niche.
          • Notifications (optional)Only after you switch them on. Turn them off any time in Alerts or your browser settings.
          • You're in controlClear history and interests in Settings. Removing the app or clearing site data deletes everything.
          • Respect creatorsOnly download videos you own or have permission to save.
